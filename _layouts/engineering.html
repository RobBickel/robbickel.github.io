After the design

The NRE ends. The device's life doesn't.

Modules reach end of life. Carriers retire networks. Firmware needs updates long after the design team has moved on. Owning systems after the build is the core of our agency, so the team that designed your device can stay with it.

See all services
  • After-build ownership. Firmware maintenance, documentation, and planning for component and module end of life.
  • Change management. Every hardware and firmware change assessed, tested, released, and recorded, including what it means for your certifications.
  • Break-fix. When devices fail in the field and the original designers are gone, we stabilize first, then find the real cause.
