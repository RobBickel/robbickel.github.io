How it works

Assess. Stabilize. Manage.

The same model a managed IT provider uses for your network, applied to your applications.

  1. 01

    Assess

    A fixed-scope Software Portfolio Audit inventories every application, integration, and dependency, then ranks risk and cost into a plan your leadership can act on.

  2. 02

    Stabilize

    We take custody of access and credentials, add monitoring and backups, write the runbooks, apply critical patches, and retire what you no longer need.

  3. 03

    Manage

    Under your service agreement we operate, maintain, and improve the portfolio, with a quarterly review so new sprawl doesn't creep back in.
