# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 30, 2026_

This policy explains what information Rob Bickel Agency ("we," "us") collects through robbickel.com and our advertising, and how it is used.

## Information we collect

- **Information you give us.** When you email us or submit a LinkedIn Lead Gen Form, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address, company, job title, and message.
- **Usage information.** Our website uses Google Analytics and the LinkedIn Insight Tag, which use cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as pages visited, referring sites, device and browser type, and approximate location.

## How we use it

- To respond to your inquiry and send materials you requested
- To follow up about our services, if you've contacted us or requested a guide
- To measure and improve our website and advertising, including showing relevant ads on LinkedIn

We do not sell your personal information.

## Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate (for example, email and analytics providers, Google, and LinkedIn), when required by law, or with your consent.

## Your choices

- You can unsubscribe from follow-up emails at any time by replying "unsubscribe."
- You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information by emailing [{{ site.data.settings.email }}](mailto:{{ site.data.settings.email }}).
- You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and manage LinkedIn ad preferences in your LinkedIn account settings.

## Retention

We keep contact information for as long as needed to respond to you and maintain our business relationship, or as required by law.
