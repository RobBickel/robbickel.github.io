# About Rob Bickel Agency

<p class="lead">We keep the software businesses run on healthy, so it stops being a risk and goes back to being an asset.</p>

Rob Bickel Agency provides managed software services: the kind of ongoing ownership a managed IT provider gives your network, applied to the applications, integrations, and internal tools your business depends on.

We exist because of a pattern we kept seeing. Companies invest heavily to build software, then leave it without an owner. The developer moves on, documentation drifts, and a system that runs billing or operations quietly becomes the company's biggest risk. More recently, AI has made that pattern faster: more software, built by more people, owned by fewer.

## How the agency is structured

**Senior partner on every engagement.** Rob Bickel leads each client relationship personally and stays hands-on: setting technical direction, making the hard calls, and doing real work on architecture and the riskiest changes.

**Onshore and nearshore engineers.** Implementation, monitoring, patching, and day-to-day coverage are handled by onshore and nearshore engineers working in U.S.-aligned time zones. That gives clients team-level capacity and continuity at a sensible cost, without offshore handoff lag.

**No single point of failure.** Knowledge is shared across the team and written down, so your systems never depend on one person, including anyone on ours.

## Senior partner: Rob Bickel

Rob is a technologist and engineering leader with more than 20 years of experience building scalable systems in fintech, SaaS, and high-growth startups. He has led global engineering teams, modernized enterprise platforms, and launched data-driven products, across large enterprises and zero-to-one startups alike, with deep experience in IoT, digital marketing technology, and artificial intelligence.

Rob grew up off-grid in the interior of Alaska, which is a good education in keeping essential systems running with what you have. He's based in Wildwood, Missouri, and the agency works with companies across the United States.

## How we work

- **Senior judgment, team capacity.** You get a partner who has done this before, and the engineers to execute.
- **Fewer systems, better maintained.** We'd rather retire a tool than add one.
- **Built for handoff.** Everything is documented so you could operate it yourself, or move to another provider, without disruption.
- **Enterprise-ready terms.** One service agreement, flexible statements of work, and monthly itemized invoicing.
