# Rob Bickel

<p class="lead">I keep the software that businesses run on healthy, so it stops being a risk and goes back to being an asset.</p>

I'm a technologist and engineering leader with more than 20 years of experience building scalable systems in fintech, SaaS, and high-growth startups. I've led global engineering teams, modernized enterprise platforms, and launched data-driven products, and I've worked across large enterprises and zero-to-one startups alike, with deep experience in IoT, digital marketing technology, and artificial intelligence.

Across all of it, I kept seeing the same pattern: companies invest heavily to build software, then leave it without an owner. The developer moves on, documentation drifts, and a system that runs billing or operations quietly becomes the company's biggest risk.

That's the problem I work on now. I provide managed software services, the kind of ongoing ownership a managed IT provider gives your network, for the applications, integrations, and internal tools your business depends on.

## How I work

- **Direct access.** You work with me, not an account manager relaying messages.
- **Fewer systems, better maintained.** I'd rather retire a tool than add one.
- **Built for handoff.** Everything is documented so you could operate it, or replace me, without disruption.
- **Enterprise-ready terms.** One service agreement, flexible statements of work, and monthly itemized invoicing.

## Background

I grew up off-grid in the interior of Alaska, which is a good education in keeping essential systems running with what you have. I'm now based in Wildwood, Missouri, and work with companies across the United States.
