# Marketing Services

Data-driven marketing that generates measurable ROI — not vanity metrics.

---

## What We Offer

### Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
Get found when people search for what you sell. We focus on sustainable, white-hat SEO that builds long-term organic traffic.

- **Technical SEO:** Site speed, crawlability, structured data
- **On-page optimization:** Content strategy, keyword targeting, internal linking
- **Off-page SEO:** Link building, digital PR, authority building
- **Local SEO:** Google Business Profile, local citations, reviews

### Paid Advertising
Targeted advertising that turns clicks into customers. We manage campaigns across platforms and optimize for conversions, not impressions.

- **Google Ads:** Search, Display, Shopping, YouTube
- **Meta Ads:** Facebook, Instagram prospecting and retargeting
- **LinkedIn Ads:** B2B targeting by job title, company, industry
- **Programmatic:** Display and native advertising at scale

### Content Marketing
Content that attracts, educates, and converts. We create content strategies tied to business outcomes.

- Content strategy and editorial calendars
- Blog posts, guides, and thought leadership
- Case studies and customer stories
- Email newsletters and drip campaigns

### Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)
Turn more visitors into leads and customers. We use data to find and fix conversion bottlenecks.

- Landing page optimization
- A/B testing and experimentation
- Funnel analysis and user journey mapping
- Form optimization and friction reduction

---

## Our Approach

**We treat marketing as a system, not a series of tactics.**

1. **Audit:** Understand your current traffic, conversions, and competitive landscape
2. **Strategy:** Develop a channel mix based on your goals and budget
3. **Execute:** Launch campaigns with proper tracking and attribution
4. **Measure:** Report on metrics that matter (revenue, not just traffic)
5. **Optimize:** Continuously improve based on data
