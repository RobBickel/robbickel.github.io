# Technical Advisory

Some decisions are too important to make without experienced technical input — but not frequent enough to justify a full-time executive.

We provide senior technical guidance on a fractional basis: strategy, architecture, due diligence, and the kind of honest assessment that's hard to get from people who report to you.

---

## Services

### Fractional CTO

Technical leadership for organizations that need strategic direction without the cost of a full-time executive.

We help with:

- **Technical strategy** — aligning technology decisions with business objectives
- **Build vs. buy decisions** — honest assessment of when to invest internally
- **Team structure** — hiring plans, org design, role definitions
- **Vendor evaluation** — cutting through sales pitches to assess real fit
- **Board communication** — translating technical reality for non-technical stakeholders

Typical engagement: 10-20 hours per month, ongoing.

### Technical Due Diligence

For investors, acquirers, and boards evaluating technology companies or assets.

We assess:

- **Code quality** — architecture, maintainability, technical debt
- **Team capability** — depth, structure, key-person risk
- **Scalability** — what breaks at 10x current load
- **Security posture** — vulnerabilities, compliance gaps, exposure
- **IP and licensing** — ownership clarity, open source obligations
- **Rebuild cost** — what it would actually take to replace this

Deliverable: Executive summary for decision-makers, plus detailed technical appendix.

Typical engagement: 1-3 weeks, fixed scope.

### Architecture Reviews

Deep assessment of existing systems to identify risks and improvement opportunities.

We examine:

- **System design** — does the architecture match the problem?
- **Performance** — bottlenecks, scaling limits, resource efficiency
- **Reliability** — failure modes, monitoring gaps, incident response readiness
- **Security** — authentication, authorization, data protection
- **Maintainability** — can someone else understand and modify this?

Deliverable: Findings report with prioritized recommendations.

Typical engagement: 2-4 weeks.
