# Consulting & Advisory

Executive-level technical guidance without the full-time cost.

---

## Services

### Fractional CTO
Technical leadership for companies that need strategic direction but aren't ready for a full-time executive. We help you:

- Set technical vision and roadmap
- Evaluate build vs. buy decisions
- Hire and structure engineering teams
- Establish engineering culture and processes
- Interface between business and technical teams

**Typical engagement:** 10-20 hours/month on retainer

### Technical Due Diligence
For investors, acquirers, or boards evaluating a technology company. We assess:

- Code quality and architecture
- Technical debt and risk factors
- Team capability and structure
- Scalability and security posture
- IP ownership and licensing
- Build vs. buy vs. rebuild costs

**Deliverable:** Executive summary + detailed technical report

### Architecture Reviews
Deep analysis of your existing systems to identify risks, inefficiencies, and improvement opportunities.

- System design evaluation
- Performance bottleneck analysis
- Security architecture review
- Cloud cost optimization
- Scalability planning

### Strategic Planning
Help leadership teams make informed technology decisions.

- Technology roadmap development
- Vendor selection and evaluation
- Build vs. buy analysis
- M&A technology integration
- Digital transformation strategy
