How it works

01 · Assess

A fixed-scope Software Portfolio Audit (30–45 days) inventories every system, maps dependencies, and ranks risk and cost into a plan leadership can act on.

02 · Stabilize

We take custody of access and credentials, add monitoring and backups, write runbooks, apply critical patches, and retire what you don't need.

03 · Manage

Ongoing operation, maintenance, and improvement under your service agreement, with a quarterly portfolio review.
