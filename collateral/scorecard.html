Software Estate Risk Scorecard

Twenty yes-or-no questions that show where the software your business depends on is most likely to fail you, and what to fix first.

How to use it
  1. Answer for your most business-critical systems: billing, customer-facing apps, operations tools, and integrations.
  2. Answer honestly. "Probably" counts as No.
  3. Count your No answers per section and in total.
  4. Use the scoring guide on page 3.

Takes about 10 minutes.

Scoring guide

What your score means

0–4

Under control

Your software has owners, visibility, and basic safeguards. Focus on keeping it that way with a quarterly portfolio review.

5–10

Exposed

Things work today, but you're relying on luck in places. One departure or outage could become expensive. Fix your weakest section first.

11–20

At risk

Critical systems lack an owner, visibility, or protection. Treat this as a business risk, not an IT task. Start with a full inventory.

Where to start, by section

1Ownership: name an accountable owner for each critical system, and document anything only one person understands.
2Operations: add uptime monitoring and alerts, then test one backup restore this month.
3Security: move accounts to company ownership, adopt a password manager, and rotate keys former staff could reach.
4Cost: review 12 months of software spend line by line and cancel what nobody uses.

A section with 3 or more No answers is usually where the next incident comes from.
